[설명 / Description]
----------------------------------------------------------------
PAWST CLASS connects volunteer flight companions with trusted rescue organizations in Korea to help rescued dogs reach their new homes in the United States.

Each year, volunteers fly from Incheon (ICN) to Atlanta (ATL) and offer to bring a rescue dog along — saving the dog from a long, stressful solo journey and giving partner organizations a safe, affordable way to complete international adoptions.

PAWST CLASS makes this process simple:

• Volunteers register their flight details and get matched with a dog in need of transport
• Partner rescue organizations (K-Pups for Love, Adopt Me Korea, 감자네 하우스) manage dogs and track every step of the journey
• Everyone can follow transport status — pending, contacted, and completed — in real time

PAWST CLASS is a free, nonprofit project built to make Korea-to-US rescue dog transport more organized, transparent, and accessible.
